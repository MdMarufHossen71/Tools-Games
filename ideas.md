# Selected Direction — Cobalt Workshop

## Design Movement
**Digital craft / editorial product design.** This direction combines a serious browser-workbench atmosphere with tactile translucent materials, avoiding generic neon dashboards and over-rounded SaaS cards.

## Core Principles
1. **Utility first:** Inputs, output, copying, and downloading remain visually immediate at every screen size.
2. **Crafted contrast:** Deep ink surfaces carry electric cobalt signals, while warm paper-like panels provide readable places to work.
3. **Purposeful asymmetry:** Hero and section composition use a left-aligned working narrative alongside visual instruments, rather than a centered marketing stack.
4. **Play as a companion:** Games use more expressive accents and kinetic forms without undermining the clarity of the tools.

## Color Philosophy
The signature **Electric Cobalt (#3264FF)** represents dependable action, focused attention, and a contemporary Bangladeshi digital identity. It is grounded by deep ink navy (#0A1025) for trust and luminous paper (#F7F6F1) for relief. Coral and mango appear sparingly as human, playful confirmation colors—never as a distracting rainbow system.

## Layout Paradigm
Use a **workbench rail**: each primary page begins with a compact left-aligned information rail and then opens into flexible tool surfaces. Categories are clustered like instrument trays with varied spans, rather than uniform grids. Tool pages pair an input bench with an output bench and a compact action rail.

## Signature Elements
1. A small diamond-and-crosshair **workshop mark** used as navigation anchor, favicon, and section eyebrow.
2. Fine **nakshi-inspired stitch lines** and coordinate dots as low-contrast background texture.
3. **Cobalt edge lights:** thin cobalt rules and inset highlights that indicate current focus, work state, and active filters.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel direct and mechanical: buttons compress slightly on press, filters slide with short directional motion, and results change instantly. Copy and download actions always produce quiet visible confirmation. Keyboard-forward paths are never delayed by decoration.

## Animation
Use 120–220ms transform-and-opacity transitions with a crisp cubic-bezier ease-out. Animate only meaningful state changes: tool-card rise on hover, a short result reveal, an opening drawer, and game-card shimmer. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no looping ornamental motion outside the hero's subtle ambient markers.

## Typography System
Use **Noto Sans Bengali** for Bangla body and interface copy, paired with **Space Grotesk** for compact English titles, tool labels, numeric results, and display hierarchy. Headlines are 600–700 weight with tight tracking; utility labels are 11–13px uppercase English or concise Bangla; long descriptions stay at a comfortable 16–18px leading.

## Brand Essence
**Tools & Games BD is the private browser workbench for Bangladesh—serious daily utilities and original mini games, without sign-ups or data collection.**

Personality: **capable, playful, trustworthy**.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and action-led; CTAs name the concrete next step; microcopy removes uncertainty in Bangla first and English second. Avoid vague promises or marketing filler.

Examples:
- “যা দরকার, এখনই browser-এ করুন।”
- “Paste করুন — ফলাফল সঙ্গে সঙ্গে পাবেন।”

## Wordmark & Logo
The mark is an interlocking cobalt game-pad cross, code bracket, and folk-diamond. The wordmark uses a compact, custom-spaced Space Grotesk lockup paired with a Bengali descriptor, never a default-font standalone name.

**Electric Cobalt — #3264FF**
